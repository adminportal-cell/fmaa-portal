# Australia’s leading body connecting students to investment banking, asset management, consulting, financial and professional services employers.

The Financial Management Association of Australia (FMAA) has branches across 6 universities connecting over 5,000 members to over 65 sponsor firms.

Student members enjoy exclusive access to FMAA keynote events, receive a weekly newsletter delivered to their inbox with all upcoming events, internship and graduate employment opportunities in one place, plus more!

 [Learn more about becoming a member](https://www.fmaa.com.au/member)
